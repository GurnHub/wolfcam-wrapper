**Alpha release.** This is the first public build of WolfDeck. It works, but expect rough edges, and parts of it have not yet been tried on every platform (see *Not yet verified* below). Bug reports are welcome as GitHub issues.

WolfDeck is a desktop front end for [WolfcamQL](https://github.com/brugal/wolfcamql), the Quake Live / Quake 3 demo player, and a replacement for Wolf Whisperer. It does not include WolfcamQL; it starts the copy you already have.

## What's in it

- **Library**: indexes your demo folders and reads every demo directly (map, mode, players, kills, chat). Search, filters, favourites, tags, notes.
- **Player**: remote control for WolfcamQL. Seeking, a kill timeline, speeds, points of view, camera tools (add, undo and edit points), looping, recording video or a shorter demo, screenshots, voice-over audio.
- **Frag finder**: find kills and multi-kills across all your demos by player, victim and weapon.
- **Clips & render**: queue clips and render them unattended, with optional ffmpeg encoding to MP4, ProRes, DNxHR and more.
- **Config**: profiles for viewing and movie making, with HUD chooser, frag messages and the watched player's name position. Settings that need a video restart are marked.
- **Keys**: view and edit WolfcamQL's keybinds, with a ready-made layout for demo viewing and movie making.

See the [README](https://github.com/GurnHub/wolfcam-wrapper#readme) for details.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. Inside the app the version shows as 0.1.0.

The installers are not code-signed:
- **Windows** SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS** may say the app "cannot be opened" or "is damaged". Right-click the app and choose *Open*, or run `xattr -cr /Applications/WolfDeck.app`.

## Requirements

- WolfcamQL 12 or newer, with the Quake Live pak files in its `baseq3` (WolfDeck can copy them from your Quake Live install).
- ffmpeg, only for encoding renders to MP4/MOV/MKV.

## First run

1. Open **Settings**, set the WolfcamQL executable and add your demo folders. If paks are reported missing, set the Quake Live folder and press **Sync paks**.
2. Double-click a demo in **Library** to play it; the **Player** becomes the remote control.
3. Optional: in **Keys**, press **Import from WolfcamQL** to start from your own binds, or switch on the WolfDeck layout.

## Not yet verified

- **Live control on Windows.** The Player's buttons reach WolfcamQL through a key press (F13 by default, changeable in Settings). This has not been tested on a real Windows machine yet. Launching demos and batch rendering do not depend on it.
- **macOS** builds but has not been run.
- **`.dm_90` / `.dm_91` demos** are decoded according to the WolfcamQL source, but no sample files were available for testing. Most Steam-era Quake Live demos are `.dm_91`, so please report any demo that shows as unreadable.
